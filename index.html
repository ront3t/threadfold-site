A Chrome extension for people who use more than one AI chat.

Folders for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok.

File a conversation, tag it, search the title later, and keep the prompts you retype. The library stays in Chrome on this device. Threadfold has no account and no server of its own.

  • ChatGPT
  • Claude
  • Gemini
  • Grok

What it stores

Folder names, tags, conversation titles, links, snippets you save, and prompts you write. Full transcripts are not stored. Export reads the open chat only when you ask, and the file stays on this device.

Languages

The panel is in English, Hebrew, Japanese, Korean, and Simplified Chinese. Hebrew runs right to left.
